About · 關於

A practice rooted in
art, healing, and culture

I am a board-certified art therapist, artist, and founder working at the intersection of creative expression and clinical psychology.

My work begins with a simple belief: art-making offers a language for what words cannot reach. Across more than a decade of practice — from correctional facilities in New York, to museums in Florence, to postpartum mental health programs serving immigrant mothers — I have witnessed how creative expression makes possible what spoken language alone cannot.

I trained at Parsons The New School (BFA Fine Arts, with minors in Psychology and Creative Entrepreneurship), New York University (MA Art Therapy), and Teachers College, Columbia University (MA Clinical and Counseling Psychology). I am currently pursuing a Psy.D in Clinical Psychology at Adler University in Vancouver. My thesis work has explored Chinese ink painting and calligraphy in correctional settings, automatic drawing as a healing practice, and the intersections of psychology, spirituality, and art.

Clinically, I am board-certified by the Art Therapy Credentials Board (ATR-BC) and licensed by New York State (LCAT). My approach integrates DBT, IPT, REBT, and IFS within a trauma-informed and culturally responsive framework — with particular attention to the experiences of Asian and Asian diasporic communities, where stigma and access barriers can limit help-seeking.

As founder of Soulverse 心球, I am building a digital mental wellness platform that brings art therapy to Mandarin-speaking communities across Asia and North America — designing tools for emotional reflection, meaning-making, and creative connection that respect cultural nuance and the dignity of human creativity.

— CV.
