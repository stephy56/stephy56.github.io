Artworks · 作品

Marks of memory,
traces of feeling

My visual practice spans drawing, painting, surrealism, and Chinese ink — exploring the unconscious, embodied memory, and the threshold between cultures.

My artistic practice has always existed alongside my clinical work — each informing the other. Through automatic drawing, surrealist exploration, and the slow, meditative discipline of Chinese ink and calligraphy, I have come to understand art-making as both a personal language and a clinical tool.

The works gathered here span exhibitions in New York, Florence, Hangzhou, and Los Angeles, from my early training at Parsons through recent collaborative shows. They are arranged chronologically, beginning with the most recent.

— I.
