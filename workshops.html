Workshops · 工作坊

Art as a shared
practice of healing

I lead workshops and lectures across Asia and North America — bringing art therapy into communities, classrooms, museums, and gallery spaces.

— I.

Workshop offerings

Each workshop is tailored to the needs of its participants — whether a corporate team, a graduate cohort, or a community group. Both English and Mandarin sessions available.

Public · Group

Art-as-therapy
group workshops

Themed group experiences exploring emotion, identity, and creative expression. No artistic background required — only curiosity. Suitable for community groups and corporate wellness programs.

Online · Bilingual

Online lectures
& workshops

Live online sessions accessible across time zones. Past topics include "Ethics in Art Therapy," "Healing Through Art," and "Journey Through the Darkness." Available in both English and Mandarin.

Academic · Training

Lectures &
guest teaching

Guest lectures and training sessions for university programs, professional associations, and graduate cohorts in art therapy, counseling, and clinical psychology.

Immersive

Multi-day
in-person retreats

Intensive multi-day workshops combining art-making, reflection, and somatic practice. Past locations include Kunming, Kaohsiung, and New York.

Corporate · Team

Team-building
workshops

Art-based experiences designed to strengthen trust, communication, and creative collaboration within teams. Useful for company offsites, leadership retreats, and interdisciplinary cohorts.

Community · Event

Social event
workshops

Bespoke creative activities for gatherings, brand events, and community celebrations — bringing reflective art-making into birthdays, openings, panels, and pop-up programming.

From the field

Moments from recent workshops and lectures across New York, Taiwan, and mainland China — group activities, collaborative drawing, mask-making, and public talks.
